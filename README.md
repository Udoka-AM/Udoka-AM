### Hey, I'm Uddy 👋

I build onchain payment infrastructure and the AI agents that run on top of it. I'm based in Abuja and have spent five years in crypto, shipping on Solana, Ethereum and its L2s, Tempo, Stellar and Hyperliquid.

**Building now**
- **[Corridor Finance](https://corridorfinance.tech)**: stablecoin rails that let apps, agents and merchants move money in and out of local African currencies. Live on Tempo mainnet and Stellar testnet.
- **[Agent Circle](https://agentscircle.network)**: a marketplace where traders deploy or rent ranked AI agents for prediction markets. Currently being built Hyperliquid-first.
- **[Nelo](https://udoka-am.github.io/nelo)**: lets any business take payments on Solana. Live on mainnet.

**Some History**
- Led product and design at Zinari Finance (now Yativo) across DeFi, NFTs and payments.
- Built SprintIQ on a Solana Foundation grant; a Nigerian academic institution later acquired it.
- Co-founded Seedless Wallet, which won the Bags Hackathon and the Umbra Privacy track at Colosseum.
- Regional DevRel at Superteam Nigeria, working with the Solana Foundation: produced a Colosseum winner and landed the QuickNode and MagicBlock partnerships. 

**Where I go deep:** smart-wallet development · MEV defense · Solana program and smart-contract security · agent toolchains · cloud infrastructure

→ More at [udokaam.dev](https://udokaam.dev)
